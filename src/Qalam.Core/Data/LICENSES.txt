Word lists and dictionaries used by Qalam
=========================================

en.txt.gz, ru.txt.gz, known_en.txt.gz, known_ru.txt.gz
  Word frequencies from wordfreq by Robyn Speer (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq),
  data licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

tg.txt.gz
  Tajik word forms collected from Tajik dictionaries and texts for this project.

hun_ru.aff.gz, hun_ru.dic.gz
  Russian Hunspell dictionary ru_RU, Copyright (c) 1997-2008 Alexander I. Lebedev,
  BSD-style license (LibreOffice dictionaries, https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries).
  Used unmodified (gzip-compressed).

hun_en.aff.gz, hun_en.dic.gz
  English Hunspell dictionary en_US derived from SCOWL (http://wordlist.aspell.net),
  Copyright Kevin Atkinson and others; permissive SCOWL license
  (LibreOffice dictionaries, https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries). Used unmodified.
